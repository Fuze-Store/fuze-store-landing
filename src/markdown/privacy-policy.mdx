## 1. Introduction

<br />

This Privacy Policy explains how **Fuze-Store IT Solutions** (“Fuze Store,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information in connection with the **Fuze Store** POS and store management platform.  
We comply with applicable data protection laws, including the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**, and **Philippine Data Privacy Act (PDPA)**.

<br />

## 2. Information We Collect

<br />

### a. Account Information

- Name, business name, email address, phone number, and password.
- Store address, business identification, and subscription details.

### b. Transaction and Usage Data

- Orders, products, customers, and staff activity logs.
- Payment-related data (handled securely by **Adyen**; we do not store full payment details).
- Technical data such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.

### c. Cookies and Analytics

- Cookies are used on our **landing and marketing pages** only to improve website experience and measure performance via **Vercel Analytics**.
- POS app do not use tracking cookies.

<br />

## 3. How We Use Information

<br />

We process collected data to:

- Provide, operate, and improve the Service.
- Manage subscriptions, billing, and account authentication.
- Send service updates, invoices, and important notifications.
- Offer customer support.
- Send marketing communications (you may opt out anytime).
- Analyze anonymized usage to improve performance and user experience.

<br />

## 4. Data Sharing

<br />

We do not sell or rent personal data.  
We may share limited data with:

- **Payment Processor:** Adyen (for payment authorization and billing).
- **Email Services:** Zoho Mail (for communication and verification).
- **Hosting Providers:** AWS, Vercel, and GoDaddy.
- **Analytics Services:** Vercel Analytics (aggregated, non-identifiable data).
- Other trusted vendors assisting in secure operations under data processing agreements.

<br />

## 5. Data Retention

- Account and store data are retained as long as the account remains active.
- Upon termination, data is retained for up to **30 days** for backup or export purposes, then deleted or anonymized.
- Certain financial records may be retained longer if required by law.

<br />

## 6. Data Security

<br />

We implement industry-standard security measures, including:

- Encrypted communication (HTTPS, SSL/TLS).
- Token-based authentication using **JWT**.
- Access controls and regular system audits.  
  Despite these measures, no system is completely secure; users share data at their own risk.

<br />

## 7. International Data Transfers

<br />

Your data may be processed in **Singapore** or other regions where our servers and service providers operate.  
We ensure appropriate safeguards consistent with international standards.

<br />

## 8. Your Rights

<br />

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

- Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
- Withdraw consent or object to processing.
- Request data export (portability).
- Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

You can make these requests by contacting [support@fuze-store.com](mailto:support@fuze-store.com).

<br />

## 9. Children’s Privacy

<br />

Fuze Store does not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18 years old. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it promptly.

<br />

## 10. Changes to this Policy

<br />

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted at [www.fuze-store.com/privacy-policy](https://www.fuze-store.com/privacy-policy).  
Significant changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification.
