## 🗑️ How to Delete a Category
<br />

This guide shows you how to **safely delete** a category. If you no longer need a category—maybe you stopped offering a certain product or want to tidy up your menu—you can easily remove it. Deleting a category is simple and does not require any technical skills.
<br />
<br />

### 📍 Step-by-Step Guide
<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Categories:** Select **Categories** from the menu list.
3. **Find the Category:** Look for the category you want to delete (for example, "Snacks").
4. **Delete:** Click the **Delete** button (usually a trash bin icon) next to the category name.
5. **Confirm:** A message will pop up asking if you’re sure. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to finish.

<br />

### ⚠️ What Happens When You Delete a Category?
- The category will be removed from your POS and menus right away.
- Any products or services in that category will not be deleted, but they will no longer be grouped under that category.
- You can always create a new category later if you change your mind.

<br />

### 💡 Tips
- Double-check before deleting—a deleted category cannot be restored.
- If you just want to hide a category, consider making it inactive instead of deleting.
- Keep your categories organized by removing ones you no longer use.
