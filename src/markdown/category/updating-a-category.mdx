## 📝 How to Update a Category
<br />
Keeping your categories up to date ensures your POS and menus always reflect your current offerings. This guide will help you easily edit any category, whether you want to change its name, color, image, or other details—no technical skills required!
<br />
<br />

### 📍 Step-by-Step Guide
<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Categories:** Select **Categories** from the menu list.
3. **Choose a Category:** Click the category you want to edit (for example, `Drinks`).
4. **Edit Details:** Update any of the fields below as needed (like name, color, or image).
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your updates will appear instantly in your POS and menus.

<br />

### ⚙️ What Can You Change?
<br />

| Field                            | What it Means                                            | Example |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------|-------- |
| **Name**                         | The name people see in the app and menus. *(Required)*  | Massage Therapy |
| **Active?**                      | Turn on if you want this category to be available now    | Yes |
| **Include in Menu?**             | Show this category in your POS and digital menus         | Yes |
| **Code** *(optional)*            | Short code for internal use (usually auto-filled)        | massage-therapy |
| **Description** *(optional)*     | A short note about what’s in this category               | Relaxing massages for stress relief |
| **Color** *(optional)*           | Pick a color to help organize or highlight categories    | #FF5733 |
| **Image** *(optional)*           | Upload a picture or icon for the category                | ![Category Icon](./images/category-icon.png) |
| **Parent Category** *(optional)* | Choose if this is a sub-category of another category     | Massage |

<br />

### 💡 Tips for Success
- You can update categories anytime—changes show up right away.
- Use clear names and images so staff and customers can find things easily.
- Use colors to make important categories stand out.
- If you’re not sure about a field, you can leave optional ones blank.
