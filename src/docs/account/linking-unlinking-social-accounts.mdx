# 🔗 How to Link or Unlink Social Accounts

<br />

Connecting your social accounts (like Google or Facebook) makes it easier to log in and manage your Fuze Store POS account. You can link or unlink these accounts anytime—no tech skills needed! This guide will show you how.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

### To Link a Social Account:

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Account Details:** Select **Account Details** from the list.
4. **Link Account:** Under **Social** Section, click **Link** next to the social account you want to add (like Google or Facebook).
5. **Authorize Connection:** Follow the prompts to log in and authorize the connection.
6. **Done:** Your social account is now linked! You can use it to log in next time.

<br />

### To Unlink a Social Account:

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Account Details:** Select **Account Details** from the list.
4. **Unlink Account:** Under **Social** Section, click **Unlink** next to the social account you want to remove (like Google or Facebook).
5. **Confirm:** Confirm that you want to unlink the account. You’ll still be able to log in with your email and password.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Linking a social account makes logging in faster and easier.
- You can link more than one social account for extra convenience.
- If you unlink all social accounts, make sure you remember your email and password.
- Only link accounts you trust and use regularly.
