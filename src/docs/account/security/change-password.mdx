# 🔑 How to Change Your Password

<br />

Changing your password is a simple way to keep your account safe and secure. You don’t need to be a tech expert—just follow these easy steps!

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Security:** Select **Security** from the list.
4. **Select Change Password:** Click on **Change Password**.
5. **Set a New Password:** Enter your new password. Make sure it’s strong (use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols).
6. **Confirm New Password:** Re-enter your new password to make sure there are no typos.
7. **Save Changes:** Click **Save** or **Update Password**. You’ll see a confirmation message when it’s done.

<br />

## 💡 Tips for a Strong Password

- Use at least 8 characters (the longer, the better!)
- Mix uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols
- Avoid using common words or personal info (like your name or birthday)
- Don’t reuse passwords from other accounts
- Change your password regularly for extra security

<br />

For more help, see the documentation or contact support.
