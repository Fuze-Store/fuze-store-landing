# 🔒 Account Security Overview

<br />

Keeping your account secure is important for protecting your business and your customers. Don’t worry—staying safe is easy, and you don’t need to be a tech expert! This guide will give you a simple overview of how to keep your Fuze Store POS account safe and what features are available to help you.

<br />

## 🛡️ Why Account Security Matters

- Protects your business data and customer information
- Prevents unauthorized access to your store
- Keeps your sales, inventory, and staff info safe

<br />

## 🔑 Key Security Features

- **Strong Passwords:** Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols for your password. Change it regularly and never share it.
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Add an extra layer of security by requiring a code from your phone or email when logging in.
- **Role-Based Access:** Only give staff access to what they need. Set roles and permissions for each team member.
- **Account Recovery:** Make sure your email is up to date so you can reset your password if needed.
- **Session Management:** Log out of devices you’re not using and review active sessions in your account settings.

<br />

## 💡 Tips for Staying Safe

- Never share your password with anyone.
- Use a unique password for your Fuze Store POS account.
- Enable 2FA for extra protection.
- Regularly review your staff’s access and permissions.
- Log out from shared or public devices after use.
- Update your email and phone number so you can recover your account if needed.

<br />

For more help, see the documentation or contact support.
