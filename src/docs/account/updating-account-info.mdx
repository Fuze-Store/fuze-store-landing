# 👤 How to Update Your Account Information

<br />
Keeping your account info up to date helps you stay connected and makes it easy to recover your account if needed. No tech skills required! This guide will show you how to update your name, email, and other details in just a few simple steps.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account**  from the menu.
2. **Go to Account Details:** Select **Account Details** from the list.
3. **Edit Your Info:** Under Personal Information, click **Edit**.
4. **Update Your Details:** Update your username, first name, and last name.
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your new info will be saved instantly.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Make sure your information is always up to date for account recovery and notifications.
- Use your real name so staff and customers can recognize you.
- You can update your info anytime as your details change.
