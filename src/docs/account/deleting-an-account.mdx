# 🙅 Deleting Your Account

<br />
To delete your account, follow these steps. Please note that this action is
irreversible and will permanently remove all your data.

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Account Details:** Select **Account Details** from the list.
4. **Delete Account:** At the bottom, click **Delete Account** button.
5. **Confirm:** Confirm that you want to delete the account. This action is irreversible.

> **Note:** You can re-login your account within 30 days to regain access.

<br />
<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Before deleting your account, make sure to back up any important data.
- You can re-login your account within 30 days to regain access.
- If you change your mind, you can always create a new account.
- Make sure to keep your account information secure and private.
