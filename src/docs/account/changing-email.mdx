# 📧 How to Change Your Email Address

<br />

Keeping your email up to date is important for account recovery, notifications, and staying connected with your business. No tech skills needed! This guide will show you how to change your email address in just a few easy steps.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Account Details:** Select **Account Details** from the list.
4. **Edit Email:** Under Email, click **Change Email** next to your email address.
5. **Enter New Email:** Type in your new email address.
6. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. You may be asked to verify your new email—just follow the instructions sent to your inbox.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Always use an email you check regularly so you don’t miss important updates.
- Make sure your email is correct for password resets and account recovery.
- You can update your email anytime as your contact info changes.

<br />

For more help, see the documentation or contact support.
