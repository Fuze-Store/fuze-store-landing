# 👤 Account Overview

<br />
Your account is your key to using Fuze Store POS! It keeps your information safe, helps you manage your business, and makes it easy to access all your stores and features. No technical skills needed—this guide will give you a simple overview of what your account can do and how to keep it up to date.

<br />

## 📝 What Can You Do With Your Account?

- Update your name, email, and password anytime
- Link or unlink social accounts (like Google or Facebook) for easy login
- Manage your stores and switch between them
- Set up security features
- Manage your subscription and billing information

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Always keep your email and phone number up to date for account recovery and important notifications.
- Use a strong, unique password and change it regularly.
- Link a social account for faster, easier logins.
- Review your account info and security settings regularly to keep everything current and safe.
