# 💳 Subscription Overview

<br />

Your subscription is what gives you access to all the features and support in Fuze Store POS. Whether you’re on a free plan or a paid one, managing your subscription is easy and doesn’t require any technical skills. This guide will give you a simple overview of how subscriptions work and what you can do with them.

<br />

## 📝 What is a Subscription?

- A subscription is your plan for using Fuze Store POS. It determines what features you can use and how many stores or staff you can add.
- You can start with a free plan and upgrade anytime as your business grows.
- All billing and renewals are handled automatically, so you never have to worry about losing access.

<br />

## 🔑 What Can You Do With Your Subscription?

- View your current plan and what’s included
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan as your needs change
- Update your payment method and billing info
- See your billing history and download invoices
- Cancel or pause your subscription if needed

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Review your plan regularly to make sure it fits your business needs.
- Upgrade if you need more stores, staff, or advanced features.
- Keep your payment info up to date to avoid interruptions.
- If you have questions about your plan, contact support for help.

<br />

For more help, see the documentation or contact support.
