# 💳 How to Remove a Payment Method from Your Account

<br />
Need to update your billing info or just want to remove an old card? Removing a payment method from your Fuze Store POS account is quick and easy—no technical skills needed! This guide will walk you through the process step by step.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Subscription:** Select **Subscription** from the list.
4. **Find Payment Methods:** Under **Payment Methods** section,  select payment methods you want to remove.
5. **Remove Payment Method:** Click **Delete Payment Method**.
6. **Confirm Removal:** Follow the prompts to confirm. Your payment method will be removed from your account.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Make sure you have at least one active payment method if you have an ongoing subscription.
- If you remove your only payment method, your subscription may be interrupted.
- You can add a new payment method anytime.
- If you have questions or need help, contact support.
