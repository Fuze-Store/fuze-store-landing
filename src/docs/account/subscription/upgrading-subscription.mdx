# ⬆️ How to Upgrade Your Subscription

<br />

Ready to unlock more features or grow your business? Upgrading your Fuze Store POS subscription is quick and easy—no technical skills needed! This guide will walk you through the process step by step.

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu list.
3. **Go to Subscription:** Select **Subscription** from the list.
4. **Upgrade Plans:** Under **Plan** section, click on **Choose Plans** to see your options.
5. **Choose a Plan:** Pick the plan that fits your business needs. You’ll see what’s included and the price.
6. **Review:** Review the features and benefits of the plan you selected to ensure it meets your needs.
7. **Confirm Upgrade:** Click **Subscribe**. You will be redirected to the payment page.
   > **Note:** Payment page is outside of Fuze Store's control and payment processors may require additional steps for security.
8. **Enter Payment Details:** Follow the prompts to add or update your payment method if needed.
9. **Success:** You will be redirected back to Fuze Store. Your new plan will start soon, and you’ll get a confirmation message.

<br />
<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Review each plan’s features to choose what’s best for your business.
- You can upgrade or downgrade anytime as your needs change.
- Keep your payment info up to date to avoid interruptions.
- If you have questions about your plan or billing, contact support for help.
