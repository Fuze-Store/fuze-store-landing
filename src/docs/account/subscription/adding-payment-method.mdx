# 💳 How to Add a Payment Method to Your Account

<br />
Adding a payment method lets you pay for your Fuze Store POS subscription and any extra features you need. It’s quick, safe, and you don’t need any technical skills! This guide will walk you through the process step by step.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Subscription:** Select **Subscription** from the list.
4. **Add Payment Method:** Under **Payment Methods**, click **Add Payment Method**.
5. **Confirmation:** If prompted, confirm that you want to add a new payment method. It will redirect you to the third party page.
6. **Enter Details:** Fill in your card or payment information as prompted (like card number, expiration date, and security code).
7. **Save:** Click **Save** or **Add**. Your payment method will be saved and ready to use for future payments.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Make sure your payment info is up to date to avoid interruptions in your subscription.
- You can add more than one payment method and choose which one to use.
- Your payment details are stored securely and never shared.
- If you have questions or need help, contact support.
