# ⬇️ How to Downgrade Your Subscription

<br />

Need to save on costs or simplify your plan? Downgrading your Fuze Store POS subscription is easy and doesn’t require any technical skills. This guide will walk you through the process step by step.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Subscription:** Select **Subscription** from the list.
4. **Upgrade Plans:** Under **Plans** section, click on **Upgrade** or **Choose Plans** to see your options.
5. **Choose a Lower Plan:** Pick the plan that fits your new needs. You’ll see what’s included and the price.
6. **Review:** Check the features and limits of the new plan to make sure it works for you.
7. **Confirm Downgrade:** Click **Downgrade**. Follow the prompts to finish the process.
8. **Success:** Your new plan will start at the end of your current billing period, and you’ll get a confirmation message.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Review each plan’s features and limits before downgrading.
- Downgrading may affect the number of stores, staff, or features you can use.
- You can upgrade again anytime if your needs change.
- Keep your payment info up to date to avoid interruptions.
- If you have questions about your plan or billing, contact support for help.

<br />

For more help, see the documentation or contact support.
