# ❌ How to Cancel Your Subscription

<br />
If you need to take a break or no longer need Fuze Store POS, canceling your subscription is simple and stress-free. You don’t need any technical skills—just follow these easy steps.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu.
3. **Go to Subscription:** Select **Subscription** from the list.
4. **Cancel Plan:** Under **Plans** section, click on **Cancel**.
5. **Confirm Cancellation:** Click **Cancel**, **Pause**, or **Confirm**. Follow the prompts to finish the process.
6. **Success:** You’ll get a confirmation message, and your subscription will end at the end of your current billing period.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Canceling your subscription will end access to paid features at the end of your billing cycle.
- You can always reactivate or upgrade your plan later if your needs change.
- Download any important data or reports before your subscription ends.
- If you have questions or need help, contact support before canceling.
