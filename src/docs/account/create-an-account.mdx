# 📝 Create Your Account

<br />
To start using your POS system, you’ll first need to create an account. It only
takes a few minutes.

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Go to Register Page:** Visit [https://fuze-store.com/register](https://fuze-store.com/register)
2. **Fill in the Details:** Enter the information for your new account (see below for what each field means).
3. **Agree to Terms:** Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions.
4. **Submit the Form:** Click the **"Create Account"** button.

<br />

## ⚙️ Field Explanations

<br />

| Field                | What it Means                                  | Required | Example              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------- |
| **Username**         | The name people see in the app and menus.      | **Yes**  | john.doe             |
| **First Name**       | The user's first name.                         | **Yes**  | John                 |
| **Last Name**        | The user's last name.                          | **Yes**  | Doe                  |
| **Email**            | The user's email address.                      | **Yes**  | john.doe@example.com |
| **New Password**     | The user's chosen password.                    | **Yes**  | \*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\* |
| **Confirm Password** | Re-enter the chosen password for verification. | **Yes**  | \*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\* |

<br />
<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Make sure the **Username** is unique and easy to understand.
- Use a strong password that includes a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Keep your email address up to date for account recovery.
- You can always edit your account details later if your information changes.
- If you forget your password, you can reset it using the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page.
