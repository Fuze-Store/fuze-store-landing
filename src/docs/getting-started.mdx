# 🚀 Getting Started with Fuze Store POS

<br />

Welcome to Fuze Store POS! Whether you’re opening your first shop or looking to make your business run smoother, you’re in the right place. This guide will help you get up and running quickly, even if you’ve never used a POS system before.

<br />

## 📝 Your Quick Start Checklist

Here’s the recommended order to set up and start using Fuze Store POS:

1. **[Create an Account](/docs/account/create-an-account)**
   - Sign up for Fuze Store POS.
2. **[Create Your First Store](/docs/store/creating-a-store)**
   - Set up your store details after creating your account.
3. **[Add Your First Product or Service](/docs/store/product/creating-a-product)**
   - Enter your first item, set a price, and choose a category.
4. **[Add Store Address](/docs/store/adding-store-address)**
   - Enter your store’s address for receipts and records.
5. **[Setup Your Store Preference](/docs/store/setup-your-store-preference)**
   - Adjust your store preferences on how your store operates.
6. **[Setup Your Store Payment Methods](/docs/store/payment-method/adding-payment-methods)**
   - Set up how you’ll accept payments (cash, card, etc.).
7. **[Invite Staff](/docs/store/staff/creating-a-staff)**
   - Add team members and assign roles.
8. **[Set Up Taxes](/docs/store/tax/creating-a-tax)**
   - Add your local tax rates if needed.
9. **[Create Discounts or Gift Cards](/docs/store/discount/creating-a-discount)**
   - Set up special offers or gift cards for your customers.
10. **[Start Your First Sale](/docs/store/pos/using-pos-register)**
    - Learn how to ring up sales and process orders.
11. **[Get Help Anytime](/docs)**
    - Access the Help Center or contact support if you need assistance.

<br />

## ✅ Ready to Get Started?

<br />

Just follow the steps above, and you’ll be ready to serve your first customer in no time!

Need help? Tap the help button in the app or visit our [Help Center](/help). We’re always here for you!
