## 🚀 What is Fuze POS?

<br />
Fuze POS is a modern, cloud-based Point of Sale (POS) solution designed to help
businesses of all sizes manage sales, inventory, staff, and customer
relationships with ease. Whether you run a café, retail shop, or service
business, Fuze POS gives you the tools to streamline operations and grow your
business—all from one simple platform.

<br />

### 🌟 Key Features & Capabilities

- **Dashboard:** Get a real-time overview of your business performance, sales, and key metrics in one place.
- **POS Register:** Process sales quickly and easily, with support for discounts, refunds, customers and many more.
- **Orders:** Manage orders, sales, returns, and advanced transactions with full tracking.
- **Appointment and Reservations:** Create reservations and manage bookings for services or events, ensuring a smooth customer experience.
- **Product & Service Catalog:** Organize your offerings into categories, add detailed descriptions, images, and modifiers for upselling.
- **Inventory Tracking:** Monitor stock levels in real time, receive low-stock alerts, and manage product variants and units.
- **Customer Management:** Build customer profiles, track purchase history, and offer loyalty programs or special discounts.
- **Staff Management:** Add staff accounts, assign roles and permissions, and track staff activity for better security and accountability.
- **Sales & Refunds:** View order history, process refunds, and track all sales transactions with ease.
- **Store Preferences & Settings:** Customize your store’s address, taxes, payment methods, discounts, and social links.
- **Reports & Analytics:** Access detailed sales reports, transaction history, and business insights to help you make informed decisions.
- **Import & Export:** Easily import or export your data for backup or migration.
- **Gift Cards & Sessions:** Sell and manage gift cards, and track POS sessions for security.
- **Multi-Store Management:** Manage multiple stores in one account.
- **Device Compatibility:** Works on smartphones, tablets, computers, and POS hardware. Responsive design ensures a great experience on any device.
- **Cloud-Based & Secure:** Access your POS from anywhere, check what is happening in your store.
