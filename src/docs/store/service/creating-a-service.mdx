# 🛠️ Creating a Service

<br />

In your POS system, **services** represent non-physical offerings you provide—such as appointments, bookings, or service packages. This guide will help you add a new service, making it easy for customers to book and for staff to manage.

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Services:** Select **Services** from the menu list.
3. **Add a Service:** In Service list page, press the **Add Service** button at the top right.
4. **Fill in the Details:** Enter the information for your new service (see **[Field Explanations](/docs/store/service/overview#-field-explanations)** for what each field means).
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your updates will appear instantly in your POS and menus.

<br />

## 📌 Tips

- Group similar services under the same category for easier booking.
- Use service duration and staff assignment fields to streamline scheduling.
- You can always edit or remove services later if your offerings change.
- Make sure the **Service Name** is unique and descriptive.
- Add clear images or icons to help staff and customers recognize services quickly.
- Use modifiers or add-ons for upselling (e.g., extra time, premium options).
