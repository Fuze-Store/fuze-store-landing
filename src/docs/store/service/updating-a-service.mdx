# 📝 How to Update a Service

<br />

Keeping your services up to date ensures your POS and booking menus always reflect your current offerings. This guide will help you easily edit any service—such as changing its name, price, duration, assigned staff, or other details—no technical skills required!

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Services:** Select **Services** from the menu list.
3. **Choose a Service:** Click the service you want to edit (for example, `Deep Tissue Massage`).
4. **Edit Details:** Update any of the fields as needed (see [Field Explanations](/docs/store/service/overview#-field-explanations) for what each field means).
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your updates will appear instantly in your POS and booking menus.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- You can update services anytime—changes show up right away.
- Use clear names, images, and descriptions so staff and customers can find and book services easily.
- Adjust duration, price, or assigned staff as your business needs change.
- If you’re not sure about a field, you can leave optional ones blank.
- Make sure the **Service Name** is unique and descriptive.
- Use modifiers or add-ons to offer extra value (e.g., aromatherapy, extra time).
- Group similar services under the same category for easier booking.
