# 🗑️ How to Delete a Discount

<br />

If you no longer need a certain discount—maybe a promo has ended, or you want to tidy up your offers—you can easily remove it from your POS system. Deleting a discount helps keep your checkout simple and your records tidy. No technical or accounting skills required!

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

### Option 1: Delete a Discount from the List

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Discounts:** Click on **Discounts** to see your current list of offers.
3. **Delete Discount:** Click the **Delete** or **Remove** button next to the discount you want to remove.
4. **Confirm Deletion:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to permanently remove the discount from your system.

<br />

### Option 2: Remove a Discount from a Product or Service

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Discounts:** Click on **Discounts** to see your current list of offers.
3. **Find the Discount:** Navigate to the discount you want to remove.
4. **Edit Discount:** Click **Edit** to open its details.
5. **Remove Discount:** In the discount section, click the **Remove** or **Delete** button.
6. **Confirm Removal:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to remove the discount from that item.

<br />

## ⚠️ What Happens When You Delete a Discount?

- The discount will be removed from your list and will no longer be available for new sales.
- Any past sales or reports will still show the discount as it was at the time of sale.
- You can always add a new discount later if your needs or promotions change.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check before deleting—deleted discounts cannot be restored.
- Only delete discounts you’re sure you no longer need.
- If you’re unsure, you can always edit the discount instead of deleting it.
- Keep your list tidy by removing outdated or unused discounts.

<br />

## 🏆 Best Practices

- Review your discounts regularly and remove any that are outdated or unused.
- Use clear names so staff can easily find and manage discounts.
- Communicate changes to your team so everyone knows the current offers.
