# 🗑️ How to Delete a Role

<br />

Sometimes you need to clean up your list of roles—maybe you created a test role, or a certain position is no longer needed. Deleting a role is simple and safe, and you don’t need any technical skills!

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

### Option 1: Delete from the Roles List

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Store:** Select **Store** from the menu. You`ll see a list of options related to your store settings.
3. **Go to Roles & Permissions:** Select **Roles & Permissions** from the list to proceed to the roles management page.
4. **Find the Role:** Scroll or search to find the role you want to delete (like "Inventory Manager" or "Cashier").
5. **Delete Role:** Click the **Delete** icon button (usually a trash can icon) in the action section.
6. **Confirm Deletion:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to permanently remove the role from your system.

<br />

### Option 1: Delete from the Role Details

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Store:** Select **Store** from the menu. You`ll see a list of options related to your store settings.
3. **Go to Roles & Permissions:** Select **Roles & Permissions** from the list to proceed to the roles management page.
4. **Find the Role:** Scroll or search to find the role you want to delete (like "Inventory Manager" or "Cashier").
5. **Edit Role:** Click the Edit button to open its details page.
6. **Delete Role:** Click the **Delete** button on the role details page.
7. **Confirm Deletion:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to permanently remove the role from your system.

<br />

## ⚠️ What Happens When You Delete a Role?

<br />

When you delete a role:

- All permissions associated with that role are removed.
- Staff members assigned to the role will lose access to the permissions granted by that role.
- The role will no longer appear in the list of available roles.
- You can always create a new role later if your needs change.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check before deleting—a deleted role cannot be restored.
- Make sure no staff are assigned to the role before deleting (reassign them if needed).
- Only delete roles you’re sure you no longer need.
- You can always create a new role later if your needs change.

<br />

## 🛠️ Best Practices

- Review your roles regularly and remove any that are outdated or unused.
- Keep your list of roles tidy for easier management.
- Communicate changes to your team so everyone knows their responsibilities.
