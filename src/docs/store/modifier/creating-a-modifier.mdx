# 🧩 Creating a Modifier

<br />

Modifiers let you offer extra choices or add-ons for your products and services—like sizes, toppings, flavors, or special requests. This guide will help you add a new modifier, making it easy for customers to personalize their orders and for staff to handle customizations.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Modifiers:** Select **Modifiers** from the menu list.
3. **Add a Modifier:** In modifier list page, press the **New Modifier** button at the top right.
4. **Fill in the Details:** Enter the information for your new modifier (see **[Modifier Overview](/docs/store/modifier/overview)** for what each field means).
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your new modifier will be available for use with products and services right away.

<br />

## 📌 Tips

- Use clear, simple names for modifiers and their options (e.g., "Size: Small, Medium, Large").
- Group similar modifiers together for easier selection during checkout.
- Only add modifiers that add real value or choice for your customers.
- You can always edit or remove modifiers later if your menu changes.
- Use pricing on modifiers to encourage upselling (e.g., extra toppings or premium add-ons).
- Add a short description to help staff and customers understand the modifier.
