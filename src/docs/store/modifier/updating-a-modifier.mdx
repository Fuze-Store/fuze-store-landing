# 📝 How to Update a Modifier

<br />
Keeping your modifiers up to date ensures your POS always offers the right
choices and add-ons for your customers. This guide will help you easily edit any
modifier—such as changing its name, options, price, or other details—no
technical skills required!

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Modifiers:** Select **Modifiers** from the menu list.
3. **Choose a Modifier:** Click the modifier you want to edit (for example, `Extra Cheese`).
4. **Edit Details:** Update any of the fields as needed (see [Modifier Overview](/docs/modifier/overview) for what each field means).
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your updates will appear instantly in your POS and menus.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- You can update modifiers anytime—changes show up right away.
- Use clear names and descriptions so staff and customers can easily understand the options.
- Add or remove options as your menu or services change.
- Adjust prices for add-ons to encourage upselling.
- If you’re not sure about a field, you can leave optional ones blank.
- Make sure the **Modifier Name** is unique and descriptive.
- Group similar modifiers together for easier selection.
