## 🗑️ How to Delete a Modifier

<br />

If you no longer need a modifier—maybe you’ve updated your menu or want to keep things simple—you can easily remove it from your POS system. Deleting a modifier is quick, safe, and doesn’t require any technical skills.

<br />

### 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

There are two simple ways to delete a modifier:

<br />

#### Option 1: Delete from the Modifier List

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Modifiers:** Select **Modifiers** from the menu list.
3. **Find the Modifier:** Locate the modifier you want to delete (for example, "Extra Cheese").
4. **Delete:** Click the **Delete** button (trash bin icon) in the action column.
5. **Confirm:** A message will pop up asking if you’re sure. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to finish.

<br />

#### Option 2: Delete from the Modifier Details

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Modifiers:** Select **Modifiers** from the menu list.
3. **Choose a Modifier:** Click the modifier you want to delete (for example, "Extra Cheese").
4. **Delete:** Click the **Delete** button (trash bin icon) on the modifier details page.
5. **Confirm:** A message will pop up asking if you’re sure. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to finish.

<br />

### ⚠️ What Happens When You Delete a Modifier?

- The modifier will be removed from your POS and menus right away.
- Any products or services using this modifier will lose the option, but will not be deleted.
- You can always add a new modifier later if you change your mind.

<br />

### 💡 Tips

- Double-check before deleting—a deleted modifier cannot be restored.
- If you just want to hide a modifier, consider making it inactive instead of deleting.
- Keep your modifier list organized by removing options you no longer use.
- Review related products or services to make sure nothing is left broken.
