# 🏪 How to Create a Store

<br />
Setting up your store is the first step to using your POS system. A store is
where you manage your products, staff, sales, and more. You don’t need any
technical skills to get started!
<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu list.
3. **Go to Stores:** Click on **Stores** in the Account section.
4. **Add a Store:** Press the **Add Store** button (usually at the top right).
5. **Fill in the Details:** Follow the steps to enter the information for your new store (see below for what each field means).
6. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your new store will appear instantly in your POS and store list.

<br />

## ⚙️ Field Explanations

<br />

| Field               | What it Means                             | Required | Possible Values                                | Example                      |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Store Name**      | The name people see in the app and menus. | **Yes**  |                                                | Fuze Coffee Shop             |
| **Logo**            | Upload a logo for your store              |          |                                                |
| **Currency**        | The currency used in your store           | **Yes**  | PHP                                            | PHP _(Not Editable)_         |
| **Country**         | The country where your store is located   | **Yes**  | PH                                             | Philippines _(Not Editable)_ |
| **Classifications** | What classifications you are selling      | **Yes**  | Food, Item, Service, Virtual                   | Item, Service                |
| **Service**         | What type of services you are offering    | **Yes**  | Delivery, In Store, Pick Up, Service, Take Out | Delivery, In Store, Take Out |

<br />
<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Make sure the **Store Name** is unique and easy to recognize.
- Select appropriate classifications and services to help organize your offerings.
- You can always edit or remove stores later if your business changes.
- Add a logo or image to personalize your store.
