# 🗑️ How to Remove a Payment Method from Your Store

<br />

If you no longer accept a certain payment method—maybe you stopped using a specific e-wallet, or a payment provider is no longer available—you can easily remove it from your POS system. Removing unused payment methods keeps your checkout simple and your records tidy. No technical or financial skills required!

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store Settings:** Select **Store Settings** from the menu.
3. **Go to Payment Methods:** Under **Store Settings**, Select **Payment Methods** from the list to proceed to the payment methods page.
4. **Find the Payment Method:** Scroll or search for the payment method you want to remove (like "GCash" or "Bank Transfer").
5. **Remove Payment Method:** Click the **Delete** or **Remove** button next to the payment method.
6. **Confirm Removal:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to permanently remove the payment method from your system.

<br />

## ⚠️ What Happens When You Remove a Payment Method?

- The payment method will be removed from your list and will no longer be available in your store.
- It will not appear as an option during checkout for new sales.
- Any past sales or reports will still show the payment method as it was at the time of sale.
- You can always add a new payment method later if your needs change.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check before removing—a deleted payment method cannot be restored.
- Only remove payment methods you’re sure you no longer need.
- If you’re unsure, you can always edit the payment method instead of deleting it.
- Keep your list tidy by removing outdated or unused payment methods.

<br />

## 🏆 Best Practices

- Review your payment methods regularly and remove any that are outdated or unused.
- Use clear names so staff can easily find and manage payment methods.
- Communicate changes to your team so everyone knows the current payment options.

<br />

For more help, see the documentation or contact support.
