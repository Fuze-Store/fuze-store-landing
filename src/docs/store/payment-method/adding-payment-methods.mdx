# 💳 How to Add Payment Methods to Your Store

<br />

Want to give your customers more ways to pay? Adding payment methods to your POS system is easy and doesn’t require any technical or financial skills. This guide will walk you through the process step by step.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store Settings:** Select **Store Settings** from the menu.
3. **Go to Payment Methods:** Under **Store Settings**, Select **Payment Methods** from the list to proceed to the payment methods page.
4. **Add a Payment Method:** Click the **Add Payment Method** or **Create Payment Method** button (usually at the top right or bottom of the list).
5. **Attach/Detach Payment Methods:** Select the payment method you want to add or remove from the list.
6. **Save:** Click **Save**. The new payment method will now be available in your store.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Use clear, simple names so staff and customers know what each payment method is.
- Only activate payment methods you actually accept.
- Double-check account details for digital payments to avoid mistakes.
- You can always edit or remove payment methods as your business changes.

<br />

## 🏆 Best Practices

- Review your payment methods regularly, especially if you add new services or promotions.
- Train staff on how to process each payment type.
- Keep records of all payments for easy tracking and peace of mind.
