# 🏪 How to Update Your Store Information

<br />

Keeping your store information up to date helps your business look professional and makes sure your receipts, reports, and settings are always accurate. No technical skills needed! This guide will show you how to update your store name and logo in just a few easy steps.

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store Settings:** Select **Store** from the menu list.
3. **Go to General:** In the menu, select **General**.
4. **Update Store Information:** Update the fields you want to change based on your store's needs. See below for field explanations.
   - **Update Store Name:** Enter your new store name. This is what customers will see on receipts and in the app.
   - **Change Logo:** Upload a new logo or image to personalize your store.
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your updated store info will appear instantly in your POS and on receipts.

<br />

## ⚙️ Field Explanations

<br />

| Field          | What it Means                                  | Required | Example           |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | -------- | ----------------- |
| **Store Name** | The name people see in the app and on receipts | **Yes**  | Fuze Coffee Shop  |
| **Logo**       | Your store’s logo or image                     |          | (Upload an image) |

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Use a store name that’s unique and easy to recognize.
- Add a logo or image to make your store stand out and look professional.
- You can update your store info anytime as your business grows or changes.
