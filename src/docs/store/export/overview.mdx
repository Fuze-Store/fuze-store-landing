# 📤 Exporting Your Store Data

<br />

Need a backup, want to move your data, or just like having a copy of your records? Exporting your store data from Fuze Store POS is quick and easy—no tech skills needed!

<br />
<br />

## 🚀 Quick Start: How to Export Data

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store:** In the **Store** section, select **Store** from the menu list.
3. **Choose Export:** On the **Store** screen, select **Export Data** from the options.
4. **Pick What to Export:** Choose the type of data you want to export (like products, customers, or sales).
5. **Download:** Click **Download**. Your data will be saved as a file (usually CSV or Excel) to your device.

<br />

## 📝 What Can You Export?

- Products
- Categories
- Customers
- Services
- Discounts
- Taxes
- Modifiers
- Product Stocks
- Areas

Just check your menu for all available options!

<br />

## 💡 Friendly Tips

- **Export your data regularly** for peace of mind and easy backups.
- If you’re not sure what you need, try exporting a sample first.
- You can export lots of different data—explore the menu!
- Need help? **Contact support** or check the Help Center anytime.
