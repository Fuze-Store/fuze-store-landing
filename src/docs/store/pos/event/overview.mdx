# 📅 Events: Appointments & Reservations Overview

<br />
Fuze Store POS makes managing all your bookings simple and stress-free! In Fuze,
every **Event** is either an **Appointment** or a **Reservation**. Understanding
the difference helps you run your business smoothly and give customers a great
experience.

<br />

## ✨ What Are Events?

- **Events** are any scheduled bookings in your business. There are two types:
  - **Appointments**
  - **Reservations**

### Appointments

- Appointments are bookings that **must have at least one service or product attached**. For example, a haircut, massage, or personal training session. You can’t create an appointment without choosing a service or product.

> **Note:** When you create an appointment, Fuze Store POS will automatically create an order for you, since every appointment includes a service or product. This makes checkout and payment easy for both you and your customer.

### Reservations

- Reservations are bookings for spaces like tables, rooms, or areas (for example, in a restaurant or hotel). **Services or products are optional**—you can reserve a space by itself, or add items if needed (like a dinner reservation with or without pre-ordering food).

**Key Difference:**

- Appointments = Service or product required
- Reservations = Service or product optional

## 🚀 Why Use Events in Fuze Store POS?

- **Stay Organized:** View all your bookings in one calendar so you never double-book or miss a customer.
- **Save Time:** Quickly add, edit, or cancel appointments and reservations.
- **Reduce No-Shows:** Send reminders so customers remember their bookings.
- **Personalize Service:** Track what each customer likes and make every visit special.
- **Maximize Your Space:** Fill your schedule and make the most of your staff, rooms, or tables.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Use clear names and details for each event so staff and customers know what to expect.
- Remember: Appointments must have a service or product, but Reservations can be made with or without them.
- Set up reminders to reduce no-shows and keep your schedule running smoothly.
- Review your bookings regularly to spot busy times and plan ahead.
- You can always edit or cancel events as your business needs change.
