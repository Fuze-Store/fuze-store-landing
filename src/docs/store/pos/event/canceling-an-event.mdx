# ❌ How to Cancel an Event (Appointment or Reservation)

<br />

Plans change—no worries! Canceling an event in Fuze POS is quick and easy, whether it’s an appointment or a reservation.

<br />

### 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to POS Event:** Select **POS Event** from the menu.
3. **Select Event:** Find the event you want to update (use the calendar or list view).
4. **Event Details:** Click on the event to open its details.
5. **Open Menu:** Click **Menu** to show the action options.
6. **Select Cancel:** Click **“Cancel”** or the trash/bin icon.
7. **Confirm Cancellation:** Confirm the cancellation when asked.
8. **Event Canceled:** The event will be canceled from your schedule.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Let your customer know about the cancellation to avoid confusion.
- If you cancel an appointment, the related order will also be canceled automatically.
- You can always create a new event if plans change again.
- Use notes to explain the reason for cancellation if needed.
