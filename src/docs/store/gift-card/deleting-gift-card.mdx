# 🗑️ How to Delete a Gift Card

<br />

If you no longer need a certain gift card—maybe a promo has ended, or you want to tidy up your options—you can easily remove it from your POS system. Deleting a gift card helps keep your checkout simple and your records tidy. No technical or accounting skills required!

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Gift Cards:** Click on **Gift Cards** to see your current list.
3. **Find the Gift Card:** Navigate to the gift card you want to delete.
4. **Edit Gift Card:** Click **Edit** to open its details.
5. **Delete Gift Card:** Click the **Delete** or **Remove** button next to the gift card you want to remove.
6. **Confirm Deletion:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to permanently remove the gift card from your system.

<br />

## ⚠️ What Happens When You Delete a Gift Card?

- The gift card will be removed from your list and will no longer be available for new sales or redemptions.
- Any past sales or reports will still show the gift card as it was at the time of use.
- You can always add a new gift card later if your needs or promotions change.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check before deleting—deleted gift cards cannot be restored.
- Only delete gift cards you’re sure you no longer need.
- If you’re unsure, you can always edit or disable the gift card instead of deleting it.
- Keep your list tidy by removing outdated or unused gift cards.

<br />

## 🏆 Best Practices

- Review your gift cards regularly and remove any that are outdated or unused.
- Use clear names so staff can easily find and manage gift cards.
- Communicate changes to your team so everyone knows the current options.

<br />

For more help, see the documentation or contact support.
