# 🎁 How to Apply a Gift Card to an Order

<br />

Want to let your customers pay with a gift card at checkout? Applying a gift card to an order in your POS system is quick and easy—no technical or accounting skills needed. This guide will walk you through the process step by step.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to POS Register:** Select **Register** under the POS section from the menu list.
3. **Start a New Order:** Add items to the order as you normally would.
4. **Apply Gift Card:**
   - Go to the **Gift Cards** section.
   - Enter the gift card code directly for the gift card you want to apply. It will show when you enter the code.
   - Click **Apply** to apply the gift card to the order/summary.
5. **Review the Order:** Make sure the gift card has been applied and the total is correct.
6. **Complete the Order:** Finish the sale as usual. The order summary and receipt will show the gift card payment and any remaining balance on the card.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check the gift card code before applying to avoid mistakes.
- You can use more than one gift card on a single order if needed.
- If a customer’s gift card doesn’t cover the full amount, simply choose another payment method for the rest.
- You can always remove or change the payment method before finalizing the sale.

<br />

## 🏆 Best Practices

- Train your staff on how to apply and check gift card balances at checkout.
- Remind customers to keep their gift card codes safe for future use.
- Track gift card usage to see which promotions are most popular.
- Keep records of gift card payments for easy reporting and peace of mind.
