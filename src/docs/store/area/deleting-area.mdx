# 🗑️ How to Delete an Area

<br />

An **Area** is a space or location within your store—like “Al Fresco,” “VIP Area,” “Balcony,” or “Second Floor.” Areas help you organize your store’s layout and can also represent a group of units (for example, several tables in one section). If you no longer use a certain area, you can easily remove it from your POS system. Deleting an area helps keep your list tidy and your team organized—no technical skills needed!

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

### Option 1: Delete from the Areas List

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store:** Select **Store** from the menu. You’ll see options related to your store settings.
3. **Select Areas:** Click on **Areas** to see your current list of spaces (like tables, rooms, or sections).
4. **Find the Area:** Scroll or search to find the area you want to delete (e.g., “Al Fresco” or “VIP Area”).
5. **Delete Area:** Click the **Delete** icon button (usually a trash can icon) next to the area’s name.
6. **Confirm Deletion:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to permanently remove the area from your system.

<br />

### Option 2: Delete from the Area Details

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store:** Select **Store** from the menu.
3. **Select Areas:** Click on **Areas** to see your list.
4. **Find the Area:** Scroll or search to find the area you want to delete.
5. **Edit Area:** Click the **Edit** button to open its details page.
6. **Delete Area:** Click the **Delete** button on the area details page.
7. **Confirm Deletion:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to permanently remove the area.

<br />

## ⚠️ What Happens When You Delete an Area?

- The area will be removed from your list, and units will be unassigned. You need to reassign any affected units.
- You can always add a new area later if your layout changes.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check before deleting—deleted areas cannot be restored.
- Only delete areas you’re sure you no longer need.
- If you’re unsure, you can always edit the area instead of deleting it.
- Keep your list tidy by removing unused or outdated areas.

<br />

## 🛠️ Best Practices

- Review your areas regularly and remove any that are outdated or unused.
- Use clear names so staff can easily find and manage areas.
- Communicate changes to your team so everyone knows the current layout.
