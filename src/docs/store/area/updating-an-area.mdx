# ✏️ How to Update an Area

<br />

Need to rename an area, change a area description, or update your store layout? Updating areas in your POS system is quick and easy—no technical skills required!

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store:** Select **Store** from the menu. You’ll see options related to your store settings.
3. **Select Areas:** Click on **Areas** to see your current list of spaces (like tables, rooms, or sections).
4. **Find the Area:** Use the search bar or scroll to find the area you want to update (e.g., “Table 1” or “VIP Room”).
5. **Edit Area:** Click the **Edit** button next to the area’s name to open its details page.
6. **Update Area Details:**
   - **Name:** Give your area a clear name (like "Al Fresco”, “VIP Area”, or “Washing Area”).
   - **Description:** (Optional) Add a description about the area (e.g., "Outdoor seating area with umbrellas").
7. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your updates will take effect right away and help your team stay organized.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Use clear, simple names so staff can recognize areas easily.
- You can always edit or delete areas later if your layout changes.
- Keep your list tidy by removing unused areas.

<br />

## 🛠️ Best Practices

- Start with the main areas you use every day, then add more as needed.
- Update area names or details as your business grows or changes.
