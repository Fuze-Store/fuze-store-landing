# 🔄 How to Switch Between Stores

<br />

If you manage more than one store with Fuze Store POS, switching between them is quick and easy. This guide will show you how to move from one store to another in just a few clicks—no technical skills needed!

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Account:** Select **Account** from the menu list.
3. **Go to Stores:** Click on **Stores** in the Account section.
4. **Select a Store:** Click **Select Store** or the card for the store you want to switch to.
5. **Completed:** You will redirect to root page of the selected store. You’ll now see the products, staff, and settings for that store.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- You can only see and manage stores you have access to. If you don’t see a store, check with your admin or manager.
- Each store has its own products, staff, and settings—make sure you’re working in the right one!
- Switching stores does not log you out of your account.
- If you have lots of stores, use the search or filter to find the right one quickly.

<br />

## 🏪 Why Switch Stores?

- Manage multiple locations from a single account.
- Keep sales, inventory, and staff separate for each store.
- Easily move between stores without logging out or switching devices.
