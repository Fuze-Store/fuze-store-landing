# 👥 Customer Overview

<br />

Welcome to your Customer Management hub! Here, you can easily keep track of everyone who shops at your store—whether they’re regulars, first-timers, or VIPs. Managing customers helps you provide better service, offer rewards, and build lasting relationships.

<br />

## Why Add Customers?

- **Personalized Service:** Remember birthdays, preferences, and special requests.
- **Faster Checkout:** Quickly find returning customers for speedy service.
- **Loyalty & Rewards:** Offer points, discounts, or promos to keep them coming back.
- **Better Communication:** Send digital receipts, updates, and special offers.

<br />

## What Can You Do Here?

- **Add New Customers:** Easily add someone at checkout or from the customer list.
- **Edit Customer Info:** Update names, contact details, or notes anytime.
- **Delete Customers:** Remove old or duplicate entries with a click.
- **View Customer List:** See all your customers in one place, with search and filters.

<br />

## ⚙️ Field Explanations

<br />

#### General Info:

| Field            | What it Means                                                       | Required | Example             |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------- |
| **First Name**   | The customer’s first name                                           | **Yes**  | Maria               |
| **Last Name**    | The customer’s last name                                            | **Yes**  | Santos              |
| **Phone Number** | The customer’s contact number (for receipts, updates, etc)          |          | 0917 123 4567       |
| **Gender**       | The customer’s gender (for personalized service)                    |          | Female              |
| **Email**        | The customer’s email address (for receipts, promos, etc)            |          | maria@email.com     |
| **Birthday**     | Helps you send greetings or offers on their special day             |          | 1990-05-15          |
| **Address**      | Useful for delivery or customer records _(see below for reference)_ |          | 123 Main St, Manila |

<br />

#### Address Info:

| Field                       | What it Means                              | Required | Example      |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------- | ------------ |
| **Address Line 1/Street**   | The street address of the customer         | **Yes**  | 123 Main St  |
| **Address Line 2/Barangay** | The street address of the customer         | **Yes**  | Apt 4B       |
| **City**                    | The city where the customer lives          | **Yes**  | Manila       |
| **State/Province**          | The state or province of the customer      | **Yes**  | Metro Manila |
| **Postal Code**             | The postal code for the customer's address |          | 1000         |
| **Country**                 | The country of the customer’s address      | **Yes**  | Philippines  |

<br />

You only need a name to get started, but the more info you add, the better you can serve your customers!

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Encourage staff to add new customers at checkout.
- Use the notes field for anything special (e.g., “Loves caramel latte”).
- Keep info up to date for smooth communication and service.
- Respect privacy—ask before adding contact details for marketing.

<br />

For more information on managing customer relationships, check the documentation or contact support.
