# 🏪 How to Add Your Store Address

<br />

Adding your store address is an important step to personalize your POS and make sure your receipts, reports, and settings are accurate. Don’t worry—no technical skills needed! This guide will walk you through it, step by step.

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store Settings:** Select **Store Settings** from the menu list.
3. **Go to Store Address:** Select **Store Address** from the menu list.
4. **Edit Address:** Enter your store’s full address (street, city, province, postal code, etc.).
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your store address will now appear on receipts and in your store info.

<br />

## ⚙️ Field Explanations

<br />

| Field              | What it Means                                   | Required | Example          |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------------- |
| **Address Line 1** | The primary address line for your store         | **Yes**  | Fuze Coffee Shop |
| **Address Line 2** | The secondary address line (if needed)          |          | Suite 100        |
| **City**           | The city where your store is located            | **Yes**  | Manila           |
| **Province**       | The province/region where your store is located | **Yes**  | Metro Manila     |
| **Postal Code**    | The postal code for your store's location       | **Yes**  | 1000             |
| **Country**        | The country where your store is                 | **Yes**  | Philippines      |

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Make sure your address is complete and accurate for receipts and deliveries.
- You can always update your address later if you move or open a new location.
