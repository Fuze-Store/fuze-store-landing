# 🛒 Order Feature Overview

<br />
The **Order** feature in Fuze POS is your all-in-one tool for handling every
sale, booking, or transaction in your business. Whether you’re ringing up a
coffee, booking a service, or processing a return, the Orders section keeps
everything organized and easy to manage—no technical skills needed!
<br />
<br />

## 🔍 What is an Order?

<br />
An **order** is any sale, booking, or transaction made through your POS system.
Orders can include single items, multiple products, services, or even special
requests. Each order keeps track of what was sold, who bought it, how it was
paid for, and who handled the sale.

<br />
**Real-Life Examples:**

- _Food & Beverage_: A customer orders a latte and a croissant at the counter.
- _Retail_: Someone buys a t-shirt and a gift card in one transaction.
- _Services_: A client books a massage for next Saturday.

<br />

## ⭐ Why Use the Orders Feature?

- **All Sales in One Place:** See every sale, booking, or return in a single, easy-to-use list.
- **Fast Checkout:** Quickly add items, apply discounts, and accept payments.
- **Flexible Payments:** Accept cash, card, gift cards, or split payments.
- **Easy Refunds & Adjustments:** Process returns or make changes to orders with just a few clicks.
- **Customer Service:** Look up past orders to answer questions or help with exchanges.
- **Staff Accountability:** See which team member handled each order.

<br />

## 🧱 What Does an Order Include?

<br />
Here’s what you’ll see for each order:

| Field              | What it Means                            | Example            |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Order Number**   | Unique ID for the order                  | 100234             |
| **Date/Time**      | When the order was placed                | 2025-07-10 14:32   |
| **Customer**       | Who made the purchase (if known)         | Jane Smith         |
| **Items**          | List of products/services in the order   | Latte, Croissant   |
| **Total Amount**   | Total price paid                         | $8.50              |
| **Payment Method** | How the order was paid                   | Credit Card        |
| **Staff**          | Team member who processed the order      | John Doe           |
| **Status**         | Order status (Completed, Refunded, etc.) | Completed          |
| **Notes**          | Any special instructions or comments     | No sugar in coffee |

<br />

## 🏆 Best Practices

- Always double-check order details before completing a sale or booking.
- Use notes for special requests or important info.
- Encourage staff to add customer names for better service.
- Regularly review orders to spot trends and improve your business.
- Use filters to quickly find orders by date, customer, or product.
