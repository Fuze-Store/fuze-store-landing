# 🏷️ How to Attach Tax to Products or Services

<br />

Want to make sure your products or services are charging the right tax? Attaching a tax to items in your POS system is easy and doesn’t require any technical or accounting skills. This guide will walk you through the process step by step.

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Products/Services:** Click **Products** or **Services** from the menu list.
3. **Select an Item:** Click on the product or service you want to attach a tax to.
4. **Edit Item Details:**
   - Look for the **Tax** section in the item’s details.
   - Choose the correct tax from the dropdown or list (like "VAT" or "Service Tax").
   - If you don’t see the tax you need, make sure it’s set up in your Taxes section first.
5. **Save:** Click **Save**. The selected tax will now be automatically applied whenever this item is sold.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check that each item has the correct tax attached, especially if you sell a mix of taxable and non-taxable items.
- You can attach different taxes to different products or services as needed.
- If you change a tax rate, all items using that tax will update automatically.
- If you’re unsure which tax to use, ask your accountant or local tax office for help.

<br />

## 🏆 Best Practices

- Review your items and their tax settings regularly, especially if your menu or prices change.
- Train your staff to check tax settings when adding or editing products and services.
- Keep your tax list tidy by removing unused or outdated taxes.
