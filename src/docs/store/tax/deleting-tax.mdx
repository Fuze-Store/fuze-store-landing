# 🗑️ How to Delete a Tax

<br />

If you no longer need a certain tax—maybe your rates have changed, or a tax is no longer required—you can easily remove it from your POS system. Deleting a tax helps keep your setup tidy and ensures you’re only charging what’s needed. No technical or accounting skills required!

<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

### Option 1: Delete a Tax from the list:

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store Settings:** Select **Store Settings** from the menu.
3. **Go to Taxes:** Under **Store Settings**, Select **Taxes** from the list to proceed to the tax management page.
4. **Find Taxes:** Find **Taxes** to see your current list of tax rates.
5. **Delete Tax:** Click the **Delete** or **Remove** button.
6. **Confirm Deletion:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to permanently remove the tax from your system.

<br />

### Option 2: Delete a Tax from the Item:

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store Settings:** Select **Store Settings** from the menu.
3. **Go to Taxes:** Under **Store Settings**, Select **Taxes** from the list to proceed to the tax management page.
4. **Find the Item:** Navigate to the item that has the tax you want to remove.
5. **Edit Item:** Click **Edit** to open its details.
6. **Remove Tax:** Click the **Remove** or **Delete** button (usually at the top or bottom of the page).
7. **Confirm Removal:** You’ll be asked to confirm. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to remove the tax from that item.

<br />

## ⚠️ What Happens When You Delete a Tax?

- The tax will be removed from your list and will no longer be applied to new sales.
- Any past sales or reports will still show the tax as it was at the time of sale.
- You can always add a new tax later if your needs or laws change.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check before deleting—deleted taxes cannot be restored.
- Only delete taxes you’re sure you no longer need.
- If you’re unsure, you can always edit the tax instead of deleting it.
- Keep your list tidy by removing outdated or unused taxes.

<br />

## 🏆 Best Practices

- Review your taxes regularly and remove any that are outdated or unused.
- Use clear names so staff can easily find and manage taxes.
- Communicate changes to your team so everyone knows the current tax setup.
