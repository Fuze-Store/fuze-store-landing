# 🏪 How to Set Up Your Store Preferences

<br />
Personalizing your store preferences helps your business run smoothly and gives
your customers a great experience. No technical skills needed! This guide will
show you how to update your store’s basic settings, like name, logo, hours, and
more, in just a few easy steps.
<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store Settings:** Select **Store Settings** from the menu list.
3. **Edit Store Preferences:** Find the section for store preferences or general settings.
4. **Update Your Preferences:** Update the fields you want to change based on your store's needs.
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your updated preferences will appear instantly in your POS and on receipts.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Make sure to save your preferences after making changes.
- Make this page accessible to the right staff members (by implementing proper [roles and permissions](./roles-and-permission/overview.mdx)) so they can update it as needed.
- You can update your preferences anytime as your business grows or changes.
