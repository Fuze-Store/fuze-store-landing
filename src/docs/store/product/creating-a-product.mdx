# 🛒 Creating a Product

<br />
In your POS system, products represent items that you sell — such as food,
merchandise, or services. Follow these steps to add a new product:

<br />
<br />

### 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Products:** Select **Products** from the menu list.
3. **Add a Product:** Press the **New Product** button (usually at the top right).
4. **Fill in the Details:** Enter the information for your new product (see **[Overview](/docs/product/overview)** for what each field means).
5. **Save Changes:** Click **Save**. Your updates will appear instantly in your POS and menus.

<br />

## 📌 Tips

- Group similar items under the same category for easier browsing.
- Use product variants if you sell different sizes (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
- You can always edit or remove products later if your offerings change.
- Make sure the **Product Name** is unique and descriptive.
- Use clear images to help staff and customers recognize products quickly.
