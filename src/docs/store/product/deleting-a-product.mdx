# 🗑️ How to Delete a Product

<br />

This guide shows you how to **safely delete** a product from your POS system. If you no longer offer a product or want to keep your catalog tidy, you can remove it in just a few clicks—no technical skills required.

<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

There are two easy ways to delete a product:

<br />

## Option 1: Delete from the Product List

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Products:** Select **Products** from the menu list.
3. **Find the Product:** Look for the product you want to delete (for example, `Ice Latte`).
4. **Delete:** Click the **Delete** button (usually a trash bin icon) in the action column.
5. **Confirm:** A message will pop up asking if you’re sure. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to finish.

<br />
## Option 2: Delete from the Product Details

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Products:** Select **Products** from the menu list.
3. **Choose a Product:** Click the product you want to delete (e.g., `Ice Latte`).
4. **Delete:** Click the **Delete** button (trash bin icon) on the product details page.
5. **Confirm:** A message will pop up asking if you’re sure. Click **Yes** or **Confirm** to finish.

<br />

## ⚠️ What Happens When You Delete a Product?

- The product will be removed from your POS and menus immediately.
- Any sales history or reports will still be available, but the product will no longer be available for sale.
- Products linked as modifiers or in bundles will lose this association.
- You can always add a new product later if you change your mind.

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Double-check before deleting—a deleted product cannot be restored.
- If you just want to hide a product, consider making it inactive instead of deleting.
- Keep your product catalog organized by removing items you no longer use.
- Review related bundles or modifiers to ensure nothing is left broken.
