# 🔑 How to Transfer Store Ownership

<br />
If you need to hand over your store to someone else—maybe you’re selling your business or just want another person to take charge—transferring ownership is simple and safe. No technical skills needed! This guide will walk you through the process step by step.
<br />
<br />

## 📍 Step-by-Step Guide

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store Setting:** Select **Store Settings** from the menu list.
3. **Go to Transfer Ownership:** Click on **Transfer Ownership** in the menu.
4. **Select Staff:** Find the staff member you want to transfer ownership to and click on it.
7. **Review Transfer:** Review the details before confirm.
5. **Send Transfer Request:** Click the **Send Transfer Request** button. This will notify the selected staff member that they are being made the new owner.
6. **Done:** Once accepted, the new owner will have full control of the store, and you’ll become a regular user (unless you remove yourself).

<br />

## 💡 Tips

- Only transfer ownership to someone you trust, as they will have full control over the store and its data.
- Double-check the new owner’s email before confirming.
- You can still access the store as a staff member after transferring ownership (unless removed).
- If you need help, contact support before making changes.

<br />

## 🏪 Why Transfer Ownership?

- Selling your business or passing it to a new manager.
- Changing who is responsible for store management.
- Making sure the right person has full control over settings, billing, and staff.
