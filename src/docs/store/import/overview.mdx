# 📦 Importing Your Store Data

<br />

Bringing your data into Fuze Store POS is simple and stress-free! Whether you’re moving from another system, updating lots of products at once, or just want a backup, you can import your store data in just a few clicks—no tech skills required.

<br />
<br />

## 🚀 Quick Start: How to Import Data

<br />

1. **Open the Menu:** Click the **☰ sidebar hamburger menu** to open navigation.
2. **Go to Store:** In **Store** section, select **Store** from the menu list.
3. **Go to Import:** In **Store** screen, select **Import** from the list.
4. **Fill Form:**
   - In the "Type" field, select the type of data you are importing from the dropdown menu.
   - In the "File" field, upload your file (CSV or Excel format).
5. **Import:** Click on **Import** button. Your data will be added to your store.

<br />

## 📝 What Can You Import?

- Products
- Categories
- Customers
- Services
- Discounts
- Taxes
- Modifiers
- Product Stocks
- Areas

Just check your menu for all available options!

<br />

## 💡 Friendly Tips

- **Double-check your data** before importing to avoid mistakes.
- **Export your data regularly** for easy backups.
- If you’re unsure about the file format, **download a sample export** first.
- You can import or export lots of different data—explore the menu!
- Need help? **Contact support** or check the Help Center anytime.
