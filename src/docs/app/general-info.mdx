# ℹ️ App General Information

<br />

Welcome to Fuze Store POS! This page gives you a quick overview of the app, what it connects with, and important info about your device, system, and legal details. Everything is written in plain language—no tech jargon!

<br />

## 📝 About the App

<br />

The "About App" page gives you a quick snapshot of your Fuze Store POS app and environment. Here’s what you’ll typically see or what data is fetched and displayed:

- **App Name & Version:** The name of the app and the current version you’re using, so you always know if you’re up to date.
- **Business Name:** The name of your store or business as registered in the app.
- **Device Info:** The type of device you’re using (like iPad, Windows PC, Android tablet, etc.).
- **System Info:** Details about your operating system and browser (helpful for troubleshooting).
- **Last Update:** When the app was last updated on your device.
- **Legal & Licenses:** Quick links to terms of service, privacy policy, and open-source licenses.

This information helps you and your team stay informed, get support faster, and make sure everything is running smoothly. No sensitive or private data is shown—just the basics you need to know about your app and setup.

<br />

## 🖥️ System Information

<br />

Fuze Store POS is cloud-based, so you can access your business from anywhere. Your data is securely stored and backed up automatically. The app works best with the latest versions of Chrome, Safari, or Edge browsers. You can also use the app on mobile devices with iOS or Android for a seamless experience on the go.
