## 📝 Create an Account

1. Visit [yourpos.com/signup](https://yourpos.com/signup).
2. Fill in your business details.
3. Click **Create Account**.
4. Check your email and verify your address.

> ✅ **Tip:** Use a strong password and a valid business email for best results.

---

## 🏪 Set Up Your Store

Once logged in:

1. Go to **Settings → Store Info**.
2. Upload your logo and fill in:
   - Store name
   - Currency
   - Timezone
   - Receipt footer message
3. Click **Save Changes**.

![Store Settings](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400)

---

## 📦 Add Your First Product

1. Navigate to **Products → Add Product**.
2. Enter product name, price, category, and image.
3. Optionally set stock quantity and variants.
4. Click **Save**.

```plaintext
Example:
Name: Iced Americano
Price: $2.75
Category: Coffee
Stock: 50
```
