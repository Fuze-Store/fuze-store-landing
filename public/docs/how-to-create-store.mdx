# 🏪 How to Create a Store1

Creating a store is the first step to start using **[Your POS Name]**. Each store operates independently with its own settings, products, and sales data.

## 1. Navigate to Store Management

1. Log in to your dashboard.
2. In the sidebar, click **Stores** or go to **Settings → Stores**.

![Store List](https://place-hold.it/240x240)

![Add store form screenshot](/docs/assets/logo.png)

---

## 2. Add a New Store

1. Click the **Add Store** button.
2. Fill in the store details:
   - Store Name
   - Business Category (e.g. Restaurant, Retail, Salon)
   - Phone Number
   - Email Address
   - Default Currency
   - Timezone
